World news, Wednesday 11 November 2026:
1. Russia and Ukraine agreed a large prisoner exchange, the war's biggest, envoy Jared Kushner said, as Abu Dhabi talks ended without a ceasefire; the envoys meet Putin in Moscow next (AP).
2. World leaders opened the COP31 summit in Antalya, with 77 leaders due to speak, down from 116 at COP28, and no US officials attending (AFP).

4. The US charged three men over alleged Iran-backed plots, including a September plot against RAF Fairford in England, linked to nearly 20 attacks or attempts; Iran denies it (BBC).
5. The US House voted 211-204 to send articles impeaching Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth to committee, shelving it; sponsor Thomas Massie was the only Republican opposed (BBC).
6. Democrat Scott Colom was projected to beat Republican Cindy Hyde-Smith in Mississippi's Senate race by 0.5 points, becoming the state's first Black senator since Reconstruction, for a 55-seat majority; Alaska uncalled (Reuters).
7. A Florida judge let Donald Trump's $10bn defamation suit against the BBC proceed over a misleadingly edited January 6 clip, dismissing his trade claim; the BBC denies liability after apologising for the edit (Reuters).
8. UK police arrested a 41-year-old US Army officer near Gloucester on suspicion of spying for Russia; he served with NATO's Allied Rapid Reaction Corps; Russia denied any link (AP).
9. El Salvador's Nayib Bukele said troops and Haitian police destroyed the 400 Mawozo gang's headquarters in Haiti; reports of up to 20 killed are unconfirmed, and its leader was reportedly absent (BBC).
10. Asian shares were mixed after hot US October inflation (0.4% on the month) hit Wall Street: Tokyo rose 0.8%, Seoul 1.6%, Hong Kong fell 0.7%, and SoftBank slid on a ¥1.1tn loss (AP).
